# Privacy Policy — IConflict

**Last updated: September 2026**

IConflict is an Android application developed by IBRA for entertainment. It simulates hypothetical conflicts using simplified statistics and does not provide real-world military or geopolitical assessments.

## Information Collection

IConflict does not require an account. The current version is not designed to collect personal information directly, and it does not intentionally use advertising or analytics services.

## Data Stored on Your Device

The app may store information locally on your device, such as recent simulation history, app settings, and your selected accent color. This information is used to provide app features and is not necessarily transmitted to the developer.

## Third-Party Services

IConflict may be distributed through third-party platforms. Their websites and services are governed by their own privacy policies. This policy should be updated if the app later adds third-party services, advertising, analytics, online accounts, or cloud storage.

## Children's Privacy

IConflict does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If the app's data practices change, this policy should be reviewed and updated accordingly.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published in the project's GitHub repository.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer through GitHub:

## Disclaimer

IConflict is a fictional entertainment simulator. Its outcomes are generated from simplified placeholder statistics and random variation and should not be treated as factual predictions or assessments.
