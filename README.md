# IConflict — DEV BY IBRA

**Choose two nations. Select a scenario. Run the simulation.**

IConflict is an Android app (APK) for exploring fictional, hypothetical matchups between nations. It compares simplified country stats against a selected scenario and generates a randomized outcome report.

> **Important:** IConflict is a simulation for entertainment and experimentation. Its scores, odds, duration estimates, and casualty figures are simplified, fictional outputs—not reliable predictions or real-world assessments.

## Features

- **26 selectable nations** with simplified ratings for Military, Economy, Technology, Diplomacy, and Resolve.
- **50 scenarios** across conflict, economic, technological, diplomatic, territorial, humanitarian, and attrition themes.
- **Scenario-weighted scoring** so different scenarios emphasize different stats.
- **Result reports** with a verdict, comparison table, short summary, and a copy-report option.
- **Light and dark themes**, preset accent colors, and a custom accent color.
- **Recent simulation history** stored locally on the device.

## How it works

1. Choose a nation for **Side A** and **Side B**.
2. Select one of the available scenarios, or choose a random scenario.
3. Tap **Start**.
4. Review the generated result and compare the ratings.
5. Copy the report or revisit recent simulations.

The result is produced by a simple weighted-stat model with a small random component. It is not an AI model, a live intelligence feed, or a validated forecasting system.

## Privacy

The current app design uses local device storage for preferences and recent simulation history. It does not require an account to use the simulation. The developer's stated current version does not intentionally use advertising or analytics.

- No account is required.
- Theme, accent-color preferences, and recent results may be saved locally on the device.
- Uninstalling the app or clearing its app data may remove locally stored information.
- Do not treat the simulation output as verified information.

Read the full [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.html).

## Limitations

- Country ratings are simplified placeholder values and may not reflect current conditions.
- The app does not use live military, economic, diplomatic, or casualty data.
- Percentages and outcomes are generated by the app's own formula and randomness; they are not statistically validated probabilities.
- Any generated casualty or duration figures are fictional estimates for the simulation.
- A result does not imply that a real conflict would unfold in the same way.
