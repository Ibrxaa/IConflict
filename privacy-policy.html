IConflict Privacy Policy Last updated: September 2026 IConflict is a conflict and strategy simulation application developed by IBRA. Information We Collect IConflict does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or addresses. Information Stored on Your Device IConflict may store some information locally on your device, including: Recent simulation history Selected app settings Selected accent color This information is stored locally on the device and is used to provide the app's features. Third-Party Services The current version of IConflict does not intentionally use third-party advertising, analytics, or account services. Sharing of Information IConflict does not sell or intentionally share users' personal information with third parties. Data Security IConflict stores its local application data on the user's device. No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Children's Privacy IConflict does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
