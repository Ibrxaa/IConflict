IConflict Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

IConflict is an Android application developed by IBRA. This policy describes the information handled by the current version of the app.

Information collection

The app does not require an account. In the current version, the app is designed to store app preferences and recent simulation history locally on your device. The developer does not intentionally collect personal information through the app.

Local storage

Theme preferences, accent-color preferences, and recent simulation results may be saved in local app storage. This information stays on your device unless you choose to share it or your device/app environment otherwise transfers it. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app may remove it.

Advertising and analytics

The current version is not intended to include advertising or analytics. If a future version adds services that collect information, this policy should be updated before or when those features are introduced.

Children

IConflict is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Security

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Keep your device protected and only install APK files from sources you trust.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published in the project repository.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer through github.com/Ibrxaa.

Simulation disclaimer

IConflict generates fictional outcomes from simplified ratings and randomization. These outputs are not verified real-world information or reliable predictions.
