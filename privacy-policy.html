IConflict Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

IConflict is a conflict and strategy simulation application developed by IBRA.

1. Information We Collect

IConflict does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

IConflict may store certain information locally on your device to provide its features, including:

  • Recent simulation history
  • Selected application settings
  • Selected accent color

This information is stored locally on the device and is not intentionally transmitted to the developer.

3. Third-Party Services

The current version of IConflict does not intentionally use third-party advertising, analytics, or account services.

4. Sharing of Information

IConflict does not sell or intentionally share users' personal information with third parties.

5. Data Security

Information stored locally by IConflict remains on the user's device. However, no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

IConflict does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's features or data practices change. Any updates will be published on this page.

8. Contact

IConflict is developed by IBRA.

GitHub: github.com/Ibrxaa

9. Simulation Disclaimer

IConflict is a fictional simulation and entertainment application. Its results are generated using the application's internal simulation system and should not be considered real-world military, political, economic, or strategic predictions.
